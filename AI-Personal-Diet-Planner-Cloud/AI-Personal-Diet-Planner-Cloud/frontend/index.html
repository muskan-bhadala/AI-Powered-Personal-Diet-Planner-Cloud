A daily meal plan, built around your goal.

Set a dietary preference and a goal, and get a breakfast-to-dinner plan you can save, revisit across devices, and export. Built as a cloud computing course project — every plan and file you save is stored server-side, not just in your browser.

How it works

1. Tell it your preference and goal

Vegetarian, vegan, or non-vegetarian — and a general goal like balanced eating, weight management, or fitness.

2. Generate a plan

A rule-based engine (with an optional AI API layer) builds breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner, plus a rough calorie summary.

3. Save, revisit, export

Plans are stored in your account. Come back from any device and see your history on the dashboard.

Synthetic demo data only. Not medical advice.
